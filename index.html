ABOUT

This isn't a studio for hire.

I already shoot for a living. This is the corner where there's no brief, no client, no deadline — just picking up a kit I actually like, opening the box, and photographing it because I want to. Video shows up sometimes too, but the photos come first.

Some shots turn out great. Some are shaky because I rushed. Either way, it's the stuff I'd keep for myself anyway.
